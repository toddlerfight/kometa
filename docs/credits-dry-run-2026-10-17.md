# Credits ladder: dry run (2026-10-17)

What the ladder in `docs/credits-sourcing-design-2026-10-17.md` would do on the live library, before
it's switched on. Nothing was written. For every issue with no credits and a GCD or Comic Vine id,
both were asked, so you can compare. The ladder itself would use the first that has any: **GCD, then
Comic Vine**.

## Summary

- **34** credit-less issues can be asked (they have a GCD or Comic Vine id). The other
  ~330 without credits are the 237 unmatched files and issues not yet out, which have no id to ask by.
- **GCD has credits for 19**, **Comic Vine for 22**; together **24 of 34** would get credits.
- Still empty: 100 Bullets: The US of Anger #4 and The Seasons #12 (out 14 Oct; only LOCG has them),
  and nine older issues GCD itself hasn't credited and Comic Vine can't be asked about.
- **Incomplete lists, re-measured after the Illustrator fix:** Metron records with no artist went
  from 435 to **44**; with no writer, 56. So option (b) in the design
  (filling missing roles from another catalogue) looks unnecessary.

## Things to look at

- **Long lists.** Collected editions and anthologies bring every story's team: Judge Dredd: The
  Complete Case Files #48 has 52 credit lines from GCD; Spider-Gwen Annual #1 has 30. That's right for
  an anthology, but long on the sheet.
- **Comic Vine's issue-level lists include cover and variant artists** (Deadpool Corps: Rank and
  Foul #1: 25). GCD ranks first, so those only show where GCD has nothing.
- **A short GCD list wins over a fuller Comic Vine one** (Deadpool Corps: GCD 2, Comic Vine 25;
  Hit-Monkey #1: GCD 1, Comic Vine 5). That's the one-source rule. The alternative is preferring the
  fuller list when GCD's has no writer or no artist.
